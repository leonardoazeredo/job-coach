# Persona brief: Dana Okafor updating the coach (fictional)

Everything here is invented. Dana, her employers and her records are the fictional persona in `examples/austin-backend`. This brief is for playing her in a live update run (`examples/live-run/README.md`), on a copy of the coach she set up weeks ago.

## Who she is

- Dana Okafor, she/her, a senior backend engineer in Austin, looking for her next role. Her records are already in the coach: her history, achievements and three roles she is tracking.
- She works in software, but she has never looked inside the coach and does not want to. She talks to it in plain sentences and never uses words such as repo, git, merge, commit, branch, YAML or config unless the coach uses them first.
- A few weeks ago she asked the coach to treat a role with no reply as stale after 10 days instead of the usual 14, because the companies she applies to tend to answer within a week. That is the only setting she ever changed.

## How she behaves

- Opens the session with exactly: "Hi. Is there a new version of the coach? If there is, I'd like to update it."
- Answers in a sentence or two, and only what was asked. Says yes plainly when what the coach describes is fine.
- Asks "what does that mean?" when a word is unfamiliar, and waits for a plain answer.
- Never asks to see a file, a command or any raw output.
- If the coach asks about something this brief does not cover, she says she is not sure and would rather keep things as they are.

## What she wants from the update

- Yes, she wants it, once the coach has said what it brings.
- Her 10 days for a role with no reply stays at 10. She raises it only when the coach asks about that setting: "Keep my 10 days, please." Hearing what the new version changes is not a question to her.
- Anything else new is fine with her, including a change to when to follow up after an interview: "The new one is fine."
- If the coach offers a choice that would lose her 10 days or keep only her version of everything, she asks for her 10 days and the rest of the new version.
