# Persona brief: Mira Hollweg (fictional)

Everything here is invented. The people, employers and numbers do not exist. This is a short brief for a second example persona, a frontend engineer in Berlin paid in EUR. It is not a full profile; it holds what a later step needs to write one, and the pasted CV text that the onboarding flow can be tested against.

## Background

- Mira Hollweg, 31, lives in Berlin, German citizen, no visa questions.
- Mid-to-senior frontend engineer, 8 years of experience, React and TypeScript, some Vue early on.
- Currency EUR. Current base around 72,000 EUR; floor 70,000 EUR; target band 78,000 to 95,000 EUR for senior roles.
- Timezone `Europe/Berlin`. Prefers hybrid in Berlin (2 office days) or remote within the EU.
- Languages: German (native), English (fluent).
- Looking because her employer, a travel-booking scale-up, is cutting its design-system team.

## What the persona exercises

- A EUR salary floor and `by_level` bands, with a gross-annual convention that differs from the US persona's.
- A frontend stack, so the discovery title rules written for a frontend search have something to match.
- A CV that carries a degree and a training section, which the backend persona leaves empty.
- A right-to-work answer that is simple (EU citizen), unlike a visa case.

## Targets

- Titles: Frontend Engineer, Senior Frontend Engineer, Design Systems Engineer, Web Engineer.
- Stack: React, TypeScript, Next.js, Storybook, Playwright, GraphQL.
- Industries she likes: travel, climate, developer tools. Dislikes: ad-tech, gambling.
- Hard no: roles that are mostly Angular maintenance, or more than 3 office days a week.
