# Examples

Everything under `examples/` is fictional. The people, employers, products, contact details and numbers are invented, and any resemblance to a real person or company is accidental.

- `austin-backend/` is the primary persona: a complete workspace (profile, config, CV, experience bank, three pipeline roles, one with a role CV) for a mid-level backend engineer in Austin, paid in USD. The tests copy it into a temp repo as their fixture, so it also shows what a filled-in repo looks like.
- `personas/berlin-frontend.md` is a short brief for a second persona, a frontend engineer in Berlin paid in EUR. It is raw input for an onboarding run, not a workspace.
- `personas/leeds-transport-planner.md` is a brief for a third persona, a road-freight transport planner in Leeds paid in GBP who has never used a terminal. It holds her facts and how she behaves while answering, so a person or agent can play her through a live onboarding run. It is not a workspace.
- `live-run/` holds the scripts and judge's checklist for playing a persona through a live onboarding run against a copy of the engine.
