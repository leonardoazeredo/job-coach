# Backend Engineer, Larkspur Freight (fictional job ad)

Larkspur Freight runs quoting and tendering for freight shippers. We are looking for a Backend Engineer in Austin (hybrid, 2 office days).

## What you will do

- Own the quoting service end to end, from API design to on-call.
- Work with product and operations to turn shipper requirements into technical plans.
- Design idempotent APIs for tendering.

## What we look for

- Python, Go, PostgreSQL, REST APIs and AWS.
- You enjoy working with many stakeholders across a large services codebase.
- Nice to have: Redis, Kafka.

Salary: $150,000 - $175,000.
