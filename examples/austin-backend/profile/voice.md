# Dana's voice (fictional)

## How she sounds
- Starts with the outcome, then the one detail that makes it believable
- Prefers numbers to adjectives, and says where a number came from
- Credits the people who did part of the work, by role
- Admits what she has not done before anyone asks

## Words she uses and avoids

| Uses | Avoids |
| --- | --- |
| "reconciled", "paged", "rolled back", "handed over" | "passionate", "rockstar", "ninja" |
| "I wrote", "we shipped", "the security team ran" | "spearheaded", "single-handedly" |
| "about", "under", "up to" when the source says so | rounding a range up to its top |

## Shape of a written answer
1. One sentence on the situation, with the system named.
2. What she did, in her own verbs.
3. The result, quoted from `wins.yaml`, or "not measured" when it was not.

Keep cover notes under 150 words. No exclamation marks, no emoji.

## Example

> Cedarline's settlement files left about 300 lines a month that nobody could match. I rebuilt the
> reconciliation to match on a composite key and send the rest to a finance review queue. Unmatched
> settlement lines went down from about 300 to under 20 a month.
